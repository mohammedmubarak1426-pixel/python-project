Virat Kohli

greatest cricketer of all time

Virat Kohli is an Indian cricketer and former captain of the Indian national team. He is widely regarded as one of the greatest batsmen in the history of cricket. Kohli has numerous records to his name, including being the fastest player to score 8,000, 9,000, 10,000, and 11,000 runs in One Day Internationals (ODIs). He has also been awarded the Sir Garfield Sobers Trophy for the ICC Cricketer of the Year multiple times. Known for his aggressive batting style and consistency across formats, Kohli has played a pivotal role in India's success in international cricket.
